Chaarvy is a modern college management platform built for educational institutions that want to replace disconnected spreadsheets and siloed software with a single, unified workspace. From the moment a student submits an application to the day they graduate, every process — admissions, fee collection, timetabling, attendance, faculty scheduling, transportation, and financial reporting — flows through one system that your entire institution can rely on.

## What can Chaarvy do for you?

Chaarvy brings every core institution workflow under one roof, so your staff spends less time switching tools and more time serving students. Here is a snapshot of what the platform covers:

- **Admissions & Enrollment** — Process new applications, manage admission forms, and track enrollment status from a single queue.
- **Fee Collection & Payments** — Record fee transactions, generate receipts, and monitor outstanding balances with a live cashflow view.
- **Academic Programs & Timetables** — Create and manage degree programs, assign subjects, and publish timetables for students and faculty.
- **Attendance Tracking** — Mark and review attendance at the class level, with rollup reports available on the dashboard.
- **Faculty Scheduling** — Assign faculty to programs, plan their weekly schedules, and maintain subject-topic-wise question banks.
- **Transportation** — Define routes and stops, assign students to vehicles, and follow buses in real time on a live map.
- **Expense & Vendor Management** — Log institutional expenses, manage vendor relationships, and keep your accounts reconciled.
- **Role-Based Access** — Grant every staff member exactly the permissions they need — nothing more, nothing less.
