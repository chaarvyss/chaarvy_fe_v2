Chaarvy is a web app that helps your college run its daily operations in one place. You can set up programs and subjects, admit students, collect fees, plan classes, record attendance, and track expenses.

## What you can do in Chaarvy

The sidebar groups features into the following menus. You only see the items that your role allows.

| Menu | Items | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Widgets for students, payments, stationery stock, enrollments, cash flow, expenses, and your to-do list | [Dashboard](/dashboard-overview) |
| **Administration** | **College Profile**, **Users**, **Roles**, **Fees types**, **Sections**, **Programs**, **Subjects**, **Books & Stationary**, **Addon Courses**, **Reports**, **Holidays** | [College profile](/admin/college-profile), [Users and roles](/admin/users-roles), [Fee types](/admin/fees-types), [Sections](/admin/sections), [Programs](/academics/programs), [Subjects](/academics/subjects), [Books and stationery](/admin/books-stationary), [Add-on courses](/admin/addon-courses), [Reports](/admin/reports), [Holidays](/admin/holidays) |
| **Academics** | **Time Table** | [Timetable](/academics/timetable) |
| **Student Management** | **Referrels**, **Admissions**, **Collect Payment** | [Referrals](/students/referrals), [Admissions](/students/admissions), [Admission form](/students/admission-form), [Payments](/students/payments) |
| **Accounts** | **Vendors**, **Expenses**, **Payments** | [Vendors](/accounts/vendors), [Expenses](/accounts/expenses), [Payments](/accounts/payments) |
| **Faculty Corner** | **Topics Management**, **Schedule Planner**, **Attendence**, **Attendence Register** | [Topics and question bank](/academics/topics-question-bank), [Schedule planner](/faculty/schedule-planner), [Attendance log](/faculty/attendance-log), [Attendance](/academics/attendance) |

The top bar also gives you access to:

- A light and dark mode toggle.
- **Internal Chat**, for messaging other users in your college. See [Chat](/chat).
- Your user menu, with **Change Password** and **Logout**. See [Account access](/account-access).

<Note>
  Access in Chaarvy is role based. Your administrator decides which menus, widgets, and actions you can see under **Administration** > **Roles**.
</Note>
