This page explains how to sign in to Chaarvy, reset a forgotten password, change your password, and log out.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Open your Chaarvy URL and go to the login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter your **Client code**, **Username**, and **Password**. Your administrator gives you these details. To check what you typed, click the eye icon in the **Password** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Login">
    Click **Login**. When sign in succeeds, Chaarvy shows **Login Successful** and opens the **Dashboard**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Chaarvy remembers the client code from your last sign in on the same browser and fills it in for you.
</Tip>

If a field is empty, Chaarvy highlights it and does not sign you in. If your details are wrong, Chaarvy shows an error message.

## Reset a forgotten password

You can reset your password from the login page. You need access to the email address linked to your account, because Chaarvy sends a verification code there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reset page">
    On the login page, click **Forgot Password**. The **Reset Password** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a code">
    Enter your **Client Code** and **Username**, then click **Request Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the code">
    Check your email for the verification code. Enter it in **Verification Code** and click **Verify Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter your new password in **New Password** and **Confirm Password**, then click **Set New Password**. The two passwords must match.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the reset succeeds, Chaarvy shows **Password reset successfully! Please login.** and returns you to the login page. Sign in with your new password.

To go back a step, click **Back**. On the first step, click **Back to Login** to return to the login page.

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your user menu">
    Click your initials in the top right corner of any page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Change Password">
    Click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your passwords">
    Enter your **Old Password**, then your **New Password** and **Confirm Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the new passwords do not match, Chaarvy shows **password not matching** and does not save the change. After a successful change, Chaarvy signs you out and opens the login page. Sign in again with your new password.

## Log out

Click your initials in the top right corner, then click **Logout**. Chaarvy ends your session and opens the login page.
