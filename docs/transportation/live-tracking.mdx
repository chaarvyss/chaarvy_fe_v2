<Warning>
Live tracking is part of the Transportation module, which is not shown in the sidebar menu and may be unavailable in your account. The screen is a prototype. It tracks a fixed set of sample vehicles, not your institution's fleet.
</Warning>

The live tracking screen is designed to show school vehicles on a map as they move along their routes.

## What the screen contains

The screen is titled **Live Status**. It shows:

- **Active Vehicles**: the number of vehicles being tracked.
- A **Show Map** button. Click it to display the map. Click **Hide Map** to hide it again.

## Using the map

When the map is visible, it shows:

- A connection indicator. It reads **SERVER LIVE** when the map is connected to the location server, and **CONNECTING...** while it tries to connect.
- A marker for each tracked vehicle, placed at the latest position received from the location server.
- The planned route line and stops for each vehicle.
- Zoom and navigation controls.

A vehicle is treated as disconnected if the map has not received a position update for it in the last 30 seconds.

<Note>
The vehicles, routes, and stops on this map are built-in sample data. You cannot add your own vehicles, filter the map, or view a movement history from this screen.
</Note>
