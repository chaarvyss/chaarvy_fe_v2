Live Tracking gives you a real-time map view of every vehicle currently on the road. Instead of waiting for phone calls or manual check-ins, you can see exactly where each bus or van is at any moment — helping you reassure parents, coordinate pickups, and respond to delays before they become problems.

## Accessing Live Tracking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Live Tracking">
    In the main sidebar, go to **Transportation → Live Track**. The interactive map loads and immediately shows the current positions of all active vehicles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the map to populate">
    Vehicle pins appear on the map as soon as location data is received. If a vehicle has not yet started its route, it will not appear until it begins moving and reporting its position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See on the Map

The live map gives you a clear picture of your entire fleet at a glance.

- **Vehicle pins** — Each active vehicle is represented by a pin at its current GPS position. Pins update automatically as the vehicle moves; you do not need to refresh the page.
- **Multiple vehicles simultaneously** — All active vehicles are visible on the same map at the same time, so you can monitor your whole fleet without switching between views.
- **Vehicle detail pop-up** — Click any vehicle pin to open a summary that shows the vehicle name, the route it is running, and the number of students currently on board.

<Note>
  Live tracking requires each vehicle to have an active GPS device or location-reporting integration enabled. If a vehicle's pin is missing from the map, confirm that its GPS unit is powered on and transmitting.
</Note>

## Vehicle Movement Log

The movement log gives you a historical record of every position update received from your vehicles, which is useful for verifying routes, investigating incidents, or auditing trip times.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the movement log">
    Go to **Transportation → Vehicle Movements** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the log">
    Use the date, vehicle, or route filters to narrow the results to the trips you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the entries">
    Each entry shows the vehicle, timestamp, and recorded location. You can use this data to confirm that a vehicle followed its assigned route.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Cross-reference the movement log with the live dashboard to verify that reported student counts match the students assigned to each route for that day.
</Tip>
