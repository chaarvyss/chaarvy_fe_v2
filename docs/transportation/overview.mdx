The Transportation module gives you a unified view of every route, vehicle, and student movement across your institution. From a single dashboard you can monitor which vehicles are running, how many students are on board, and where each vehicle is right now — so you can respond quickly to any situation on the road.

## Transportation Dashboard

The dashboard table lists every active route currently in operation and refreshes automatically as vehicles move through their routes. Each row gives you the information you need at a glance.

| Column | What it shows | What to click for |
|---|---|---|
| **Route Name** | The name of the active route | Stop sequence and boarding points |
| **Vehicle** | The vehicle assigned to the route | Full vehicle details |
| **Students on Board** | Number of students currently on the vehicle | The student list for that vehicle |
| **Current Location** | Last reported position of the vehicle | — |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Transportation Dashboard">
    Navigate to **Transportation** in the main sidebar. The dashboard loads automatically and displays all active routes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View route details">
    Click any **Route Name** in the table. The Route Details panel opens and shows each stop on the route along with its boarding points.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View vehicle details">
    Click the **Vehicle** name in any row. The Vehicle Details panel shows the registration number, type, capacity, and assigned driver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See which students are on board">
    Click the **Students on Board** count in any row. The student list opens and shows every student currently riding that vehicle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting Up Routes

Before vehicles appear on the dashboard, you need to configure at least one route and its associated boarding points in the Admin section.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a route">
    Go to **Admin → Routes**. Click **Add Route**, enter the route name and any relevant details, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add boarding points">
    Go to **Admin → Boarding Points**. Add each stop where students board or alight, then link those points to the appropriate route.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Routes and boarding points you configure in Admin appear automatically in the Transportation Dashboard once vehicles begin their runs.
</Info>
