<Warning>
The Transportation module is not shown in the sidebar menu and may be unavailable in your account. Its screens are still in development. They show sample data or empty lists, and they do not save or load your institution's records yet.
</Warning>

The Transportation module is intended to help you manage school vehicles, drivers, transport vendors, and routes. This page describes what the module's screens currently contain, so you know what to expect if your account has access to them.

## Transportation dashboard

The dashboard screen is titled **Dashboard - Transportation**. It shows summary cards and a status table.

### Summary cards

| Card | Details shown |
|---|---|
| **Vechicles in Operation** | **Completed** and **Pending** counts |
| **Drivers** | **Active**, **Inactive**, **On Leave**, and **Contractual** counts |
| **Vendors** | **Active** and **Inactive** counts |
| **Passengers** | A chart split into **Paid**, **Close to Due**, and **Overdue** |

<Note>
The numbers on these cards are placeholder values. They do not reflect your institution's data.
</Note>

### Current transportation status table

The table lists routes with the following columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **S.No** | Row number |
| **Route** | Route name |
| **Vehicle** | Vehicle assigned to the route |
| **Students Onboard** | Students on board compared to capacity, for example 15/20 |
| **Current Location** | Last known location of the vehicle |

You can click some cells to open a detail window:

- Click a **Route** to open **Route Details**.
- Click a **Vehicle** to open **Vehicle Details**. This window is designed to show **Vehicle Number**, **Driver Name**, **Driver Contact**, **Route**, **Students Onboard**, and **Current Location**.
- Click a **Students Onboard** value to open **Students in Route**.

<Note>
The rows in this table are sample entries. The detail windows may show a "not found" message because the sample rows are not linked to real records.
</Note>
