<Warning>
These screens are part of the Transportation module, which is not shown in the sidebar menu and may be unavailable in your account. They are still in development. The lists are always empty, and you cannot add or save vehicles, drivers, or vendors yet.
</Warning>

The Transportation module includes three list screens: **Vehicles**, **Drivers**, and **Vendors**. They share the same layout.

## Common layout

Each screen shows:

- Two summary counts at the top:
  - **Vehicles**: **Total Vehicles** and **Active Vehicles**
  - **Drivers**: **Total Drivers** and **Active Drivers**
  - **Vendors**: **Total Vendors** and **Active Vendors**
- A filter icon that opens a **Filters** panel with a search field and a status filter. The only status option is **Active**.
- A table with a name column (**Vehicle**, **Driver**, or **Vendor**), **Status**, **Phone**, and an edit icon.

Because the lists have no data, each table shows an empty message such as **No Vehicles Found**, **No Drivers Found**, or **No Vendors Found**.

## Vendor form

The **Vendors** screen has an **Add Vendor** button. It opens the **Add Vendor** panel with these fields:

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Vehicle vendor name** | Required |
| **Contact number** | Required |
| **Email** | Required. Must be a valid email address. |

The panel also includes an address section and a **Save** button.

<Note>
Clicking **Save** checks the required fields but does not store the vendor. The vendor does not appear in the list afterward.
</Note>

<Tip>
To record vendors for your general expenses today, use **Accounts** > **Vendors**. See [Vendors](/accounts/vendors).
</Tip>
