The Chaarvy dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in, and it is designed to give you an instant, real-time picture of your institution's health. Rather than digging through separate modules for each data point, the dashboard surfaces the metrics that matter most — all in one glance. Every widget on the dashboard reflects live data, so you can trust that what you see is always up to date.

## Dashboard Widgets

The widgets displayed on your dashboard depend on the permissions assigned to your role. The table below describes every available widget and what it shows.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Student Count** | The total number of students currently enrolled in your institution. Click the widget to drill into the full student list. |
| **Payments** | A summary of fee payments received, including total collected and any outstanding balances. Links through to the Accounts module. |
| **Stationary Stock** | Current stock levels for stationery and supplies tracked in your inventory, helping you spot shortages early. |
| **Student Enrollments** | A trend chart of new enrollments over time, so you can track intake momentum across admission cycles. |
| **Cashflow** | A visual breakdown of income versus outgoings for the current period, giving finance staff a fast read on institutional liquidity. |
| **Expenses** | Total expenses logged in the Accounts module for the current month, with a link to the full expense register. |
| **To-Do** | Your personal task list. Create, update, and delete items to manage your daily workload without leaving the dashboard. |
| **Attendance** | Today's attendance rate across all active classes. Colour coding highlights classes that fall below your institution's threshold. |
| **Calendar** | Upcoming academic events, fee deadlines, and institutional milestones drawn from the platform's shared calendar. |

<Tip>
  If you do not see a widget that is described above, it may be hidden because your role does not have permission to view that data. Ask your system administrator to review your role configuration under **Admin → Roles**.
</Tip>

## Managing Your To-Do List

The **To-Do** widget is a lightweight personal task manager built directly into your dashboard. You do not need a separate app to keep track of daily action items — everything lives alongside your institutional data.

### Create a to-do item

1. Locate the **To-Do** widget on your dashboard.
2. Click the **Add** button (the **+** icon) inside the widget.
3. Type your task description in the input field that appears.
4. Press **Enter** or click **Save** to add the item to your list.

### Update a to-do item

1. Click the task text you want to edit.
2. Modify the description directly in the inline editor.
3. Click **Save** (or press **Enter**) to confirm the change.

You can also mark a task as complete by clicking the checkbox to its left. Completed tasks are visually struck through so you can see your progress at a glance.

### Delete a to-do item

1. Hover over the task you want to remove.
2. Click the **Delete** icon (trash can) that appears to the right of the item.
3. The item is removed immediately — there is no confirmation prompt, so double-check before deleting.

<Warning>
  Deleted to-do items cannot be recovered. If you accidentally remove a task, you will need to recreate it manually.
</Warning>

## Navigating from the Dashboard

Every widget on the dashboard is interactive. Clicking a widget title or its embedded link takes you directly to the relevant module, so you can go from a high-level metric to the underlying detail in a single click. For example:

- Click **Payments** to open the Accounts fee register.
- Click **Student Count** to open the full Student Management list.
- Click **Attendance** to see the class-by-class breakdown in the Academics module.

This makes the dashboard your natural starting point for every working session — check the numbers, spot anything that needs attention, and jump straight to the right place.

<Note>
  Dashboard data refreshes automatically in real time. You do not need to reload the page to see updated figures — widgets reflect the latest state of your institution as soon as changes are saved anywhere in the platform.
</Note>
