The dashboard is the first page you see after you sign in. To open it at any time, click **Dashboard** in the sidebar. It shows a set of widgets that summarize key data for your college.

## Dashboard widgets

Each widget appears only if your role has permission to view it. If your role allows none of them, the dashboard shows **No items to show**.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Todo** | Your personal to-do list. See [Manage your to-do list](#manage-your-to-do-list). |
| **Students** | A pie chart of students split into **Male** and **Female**. |
| **Payments** | A horizontal stacked bar chart that summarizes payments. |
| **Stationary stock** | A table of low stock books and stationery items, with the columns **#**, **Item**, and **Current Stock**. |
| **Student enrollments** | A treemap of enrolled students. Boxes are grouped by program, segment, and medium, and each box is a section. Hover over a box to see **Total Students**, **Boys**, and **Girls**. |
| **CashFlow** | An area chart that compares **Payments Accepted** with **Expenses** over a period. |
| **Expenses** | An area chart of expenses over a period. |

<Tip>
  If you expect to see a widget that is missing, ask your administrator to check your role under **Administration** > **Roles**. See [Users and roles](/admin/users-roles).
</Tip>

## Change the period on cash flow and expenses

The **CashFlow** and **Expenses** widgets start with the current month. Each widget shows the totals for the selected period above the chart, such as **Total Payments Accepted** and **Total Expenses**.

- **Date range:** Click the date field and select a start date and an end date. The chart updates after you pick both dates.
- **Previous and next arrows:** Move the period back or forward by one week, month, or year, based on the selected step.
- **Step menu:** Choose **Weekly Step**, **Monthly Step**, or **Yearly Step**. This resets the period to the current week, month, or year.

The chart groups data by day for ranges up to 60 days, by month for ranges up to a year, and by year for longer ranges. Hover over the chart to see the amounts for a point. If there is nothing to show, the widget displays **No data available for this period.**

## Customize the layout

You can arrange the dashboard to suit how you work. Chaarvy saves your layout in your browser, so it applies only on the browser and device you use.

- **Move a widget:** Drag it by its title.
- **Resize a widget:** Drag its bottom right corner.
- **Hide a widget:** Hover over the widget and click the close icon in its top right corner.
- **Restore a hidden widget:** Hidden widgets are listed next to **Hidden Widgets:** at the top of the dashboard. Click the matching **+ Add** button, for example **+ Add Todo**.
- **Reset the layout:** Click **Reset** at the top right of the dashboard to restore the default positions, sizes, and widgets.

## Manage your to-do list

The **Todo** widget is a personal task list. Items are color coded by priority: **High** is red, **Medium** is orange, and **Low** is green. If you have no items, the widget shows **No todos. No need to hurry!**

### Add a to-do item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the **Todo** widget, click **New**. The **Todo Details** form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter the **Title** and **Description**, select a **Priority** (**Low**, **Medium**, or **High**), and choose a **Due Date**. All fields are required. The due date can be from today up to two years ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the item">
    Click **Add Todo**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can have up to 10 to-do items. The **New** button is disabled when you reach this limit. Delete an item to add a new one.
</Note>

### Edit a to-do item

1. Click the title or description of the item. The **Todo Details** form opens.
2. Change the details.
3. Click **Update Todo**.

### Mark an item as done

Select the checkbox next to the item. Chaarvy strikes through the title and dims the item. Clear the checkbox to mark it as not done.

### Delete a to-do item

Click the trash icon on the item. Chaarvy deletes it right away, without asking you to confirm.

<Warning>
  You cannot recover a deleted to-do item.
</Warning>
