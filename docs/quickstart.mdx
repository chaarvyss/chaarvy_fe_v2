This guide walks you through the basic setup order in Chaarvy. You sign in, complete your college profile, create programs and subjects, and then add your first student admission.

<Note>
  Menus and buttons appear only if your role allows them. If you cannot see an item described here, ask your administrator to check your role under **Administration** > **Roles**.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open your Chaarvy URL and go to the login page. Enter your **Client code**, **Username**, and **Password**, then click **Login**.

    After you sign in, Chaarvy opens the **Dashboard**. For details, including how to reset a forgotten password, see [Account access](/account-access).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the dashboard">
    The dashboard shows widgets such as **Todo**, **Students**, **Payments**, **Stationary stock**, **Student enrollments**, **CashFlow**, and **Expenses**. You only see the widgets your role allows. You can drag, resize, hide, and restore widgets. See [Dashboard](/dashboard-overview).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your college profile">
    Go to **Administration** > **College Profile**.

    1. On the **College Details** tab, fill in the college name, campus name, contact numbers, UDISE number, and Jananabhumi number. The college code is read only.
    2. Click **Update Profile** to save your changes.
    3. On the **Address** tab, fill in **Door No**, **House / Apartment**, **Street**, **Village / City**, **State**, **District**, and **Pincode**. **Landmark** is optional. Click **Submit**.
    4. To add a logo, click the pencil icon on the logo image and choose a PNG or JPEG file. Then click the save icon that appears next to it.

    See [College profile](/admin/college-profile) for more details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create programs">
    Go to **Administration** > **Programs** and click **Create Program**.

    1. Enter the **Program name**.
    2. Select at least one segment checkbox.
    3. Click **Create Program**.

    The program appears in the list with its status. Use the **Actions** menu on a row to **View** or **Edit** the program, or to manage its **Books Details** and **Fees details**. See [Programs](/academics/programs).

    <Tip>
      Set up fee types under **Administration** > **Fees types** and sections under **Administration** > **Sections** so they are ready when you configure programs. See [Fee types](/admin/fees-types) and [Sections](/admin/sections).
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add and assign subjects">
    Go to **Administration** > **Subjects**.

    1. Click **Add Subject**, enter the **Subject Name**, and click **Submit**.
    2. Click **Assign Subjects** to link subjects to programs using **Select Programs** and **Select Subjects**.

    See [Subjects](/academics/subjects).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first admission">
    Go to **Student Management** > **Admissions** and click **New Admission**.

    The admission form has these tabs: **Student Base Details**, **Student Details**, **Student Address**, **ADDON Courses**, and **Fees Details**. Complete each tab to finish the admission. See [Admission form](/students/admission-form).

    To add many students at once, use **Bulk Upload** on the **Admissions** page. See [Admissions](/students/admissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>
