Getting your institution up and running on Chaarvy is straightforward. This guide walks you through the five essential steps — from your first login to enrolling your first student — so you can experience the full workflow before diving into individual modules.

<Note>
  Some features and menu items may not be visible to you depending on the role and permissions assigned to your account. If you cannot see a step described below, contact your system administrator to review your access.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In">
    Open your institution's Chaarvy URL in any modern web browser and navigate to the `/login` page. Enter the **username** and **password** provided by your administrator, then click **Sign In**.

    Once authenticated, Chaarvy redirects you to the **Dashboard** at `/dashboard`. The dashboard gives you an instant snapshot of your institution — enrollment counts, recent payments, attendance figures, and your personal to-do list — all in one place.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the Dashboard">
    Take a moment to familiarise yourself with the dashboard before making any changes. You will see a set of widgets that surface the metrics most relevant to your role:

    - **Student Count** — total active students currently enrolled.
    - **Payments** — a summary of fees collected and amounts outstanding.
    - **Attendance** — today's attendance rate across all classes.
    - **Cashflow & Expenses** — a high-level view of income versus spending.
    - **To-Do** — your personal task list to keep daily work on track.
    - **Calendar** — upcoming academic events and deadlines.

    Each widget updates in real time, so the numbers you see always reflect the current state of your institution.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Institution Profile">
    Before adding students or programs, make sure your institution's details are correct. Navigate to **College Profile** from the main menu and fill in:

    - **Institution name** — the official name that will appear on reports and receipts.
    - **Logo** — upload your institution's logo (PNG or JPEG, recommended size 200 × 200 px).
    - **Address** — the full postal address used on official documents.

    Click **Save** when you are done. These details appear on admission forms, fee receipts, and printed reports throughout the platform.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Programs and Subjects">
    With your profile saved, you are ready to build your academic catalogue.

    1. Go to **Admin → Programs** and click **Add Program**. Enter the program name, duration, and any other required fields, then save.
    2. Once your programs exist, go to **Admin → Subjects** and add the subjects that belong to each program. Assigning subjects to programs lets Chaarvy automatically populate timetables and attendance sheets.

    Repeat this process for every program your institution offers. You can always come back to add or edit programs and subjects as your catalogue grows.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enroll Your First Student">
    You are now ready to bring in your first student. Navigate to **Student Management → Admissions** and click **New Admission**.

    The admission form collects the student's personal details, contact information, and the program they are enrolling in. Fill in all required fields and click **Submit**. The student record is created immediately and the enrollment count on your dashboard updates in real time.

    From this point you can assign the student to a batch, generate a fee challan, and add them to a transport route — all from within their student profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's next?

Now that your institution is set up and your first student is enrolled, explore the rest of Chaarvy at your own pace:

- **[Dashboard Overview](/dashboard-overview)** — understand every widget and metric on your home screen.
- **[Student Management](/students/admissions)** — bulk-import students, manage documents, and track application status.
- **[Academics](/academics/programs)** — publish timetables and monitor class-level attendance.
- **[Faculty Tools](/faculty/schedule-planner)** — assign faculty, plan schedules, and manage question banks.
- **[Transportation](/transportation/overview)** — set up routes and track vehicles live.
