Chaarvy separates the act of *taking* attendance from the act of *reviewing* it. When you mark students present or absent during a live class, you use the **Attendance Register**. The **Attendance Log**, covered here, is where you go after the fact — to browse past sessions, check completion rates, and verify the student-level record for any class you have taught.

## Accessing the Attendance Log

Navigate to **Faculty → Attendance Log** in the left sidebar. The page loads a table listing every attendance session that has been recorded under your account, ordered from most recent to oldest.

Each row in the table represents one period-session and shows:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The date the session was held |
| **Period** | The period slot (e.g., Period 3, 09:45 – 10:30) |
| **Program** | The academic programme of the class |
| **Segment** | The year or semester |
| **Medium** | The language of instruction |
| **Section** | The student section |
| **Status** | **Saved** (finalised) or **Draft** (not yet submitted) |
| **Recorded By** | The faculty member who marked attendance |
| **Attendance %** | A visual indicator of the percentage of students marked present |

## Viewing a Session's Details

Clicking any row in the log opens the full **Attendance Register** for that session. From there you can see:

- A **colour-coded completion bar** at the top that summarises present, absent, and pending counts at a glance — green for present, red for absent, and amber for pending — along with the exact totals (e.g., *Present: 28 · Absent: 4 · Pending: 2*).
- A **student-level breakdown** listing each enrolled student and their attendance status for that session. Use this view to verify a specific student's record or resolve a dispute about whether someone was marked correctly.

<Note>
  Clicking a row takes you to the Attendance Register in read mode for that session. To make changes, the session must still be in **Draft** status. Saved sessions are locked and cannot be edited from the log.
</Note>

## Filtering and Finding Records

If you have a long history of sessions, use the **Filter** panel to narrow the list down quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filter panel">
    Click the **Filter** button at the top-right of the Attendance Log page. A filter drawer slides in from the side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your filters">
    You can filter by any combination of:
    - **Date range** — enter a start and end date to view only sessions within that window
    - **Program** — limit results to a specific academic programme
    - **Segment** — narrow to a particular year or semester
    - **Medium** — filter by language of instruction
    - **Section** — show only sessions for a specific student section
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the results">
    Click **Apply** to refresh the table with your selected filters. To start over, click **Reset Filters** to return to the full unfiltered list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The Attendance Log is for reviewing history only — you cannot add new attendance entries here. To mark attendance for a current or past class, go to the **Attendance Register** under **Academics → Attendance**.
</Tip>
