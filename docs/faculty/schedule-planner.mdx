The Schedule Planner gives you a clear, calendar-based view of your entire teaching timeline. From a single screen, you can plan upcoming sessions, update existing entries when your schedule changes, and generate ready-to-print question papers drawn directly from your topic question bank — all without leaving the planner.

## Viewing Your Schedule

Navigate to **Faculty → Schedule Planner** to open the calendar view. You can switch between **Week** and **Month** views using the toggle in the top-right of the calendar, and use the arrow buttons to navigate forward or backward. A coverage bar at the top of the page summarises how many topics are completed, pending, or overdue across your planned sessions.

Cells that already have a planned session display the subject and topic at a glance, so you can see your upcoming workload across the week or month at once. Completed sessions are visually distinguished from upcoming ones, making it easy to track your progress through the syllabus.

## Planning a New Session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the planning panel">
    Click the **+ Plan Schedule** button at the top of the page, or click directly on an empty cell in the calendar for the date and period you want to schedule. The **Plan New Schedule** panel slides open on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the session details">
    In the panel, fill in the following required fields:
    - **Date** — the date of the session (Sundays and holidays are automatically disabled)
    - **Period** — the period slot for the session (e.g., Period 2, 09:00 – 09:45)
    - **Program** — the academic programme this session belongs to
    - **Segment** — the year or semester within that programme
    - **Subject** — the subject you will teach
    - **Medium** — the language of instruction
    - **Section** — the student section attending this session
    - **Topic** — the specific topic from the subject's syllabus (searchable)

    If your timetable is already configured, some fields may be pre-filled automatically based on your assigned timetable. You can edit any pre-filled value before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the session">
    Click **Add Schedule** to add the session to the calendar. The cell updates immediately to show the subject and topic you just planned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating a Scheduled Session

If you need to change any detail of an already-planned session — for example, swapping a date or period — you can edit it directly from the calendar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the existing entry">
    On the calendar, click the cell of the session you want to edit. The **Schedule Details** panel opens, showing the session's current date, period, topic, and completion status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update the **Date** or **Period** fields as needed. You can also toggle the session's completion status using the **Mark Complete** or **Mark Pending** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Done** to close the panel. Changes are saved as you make them. To permanently remove the session from your schedule, click **Delete Schedule** at the bottom of the panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generating a Question Paper

The Schedule Planner integrates a Question Paper Generator that pulls questions directly from your topic question banks. Use it to compose and print exam papers without switching to a separate tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Question Paper Generator">
    From within the Schedule Planner, click the **Question Paper Generator** option. The generator opens with a list of topics drawn from your planned sessions for the selected subject.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select topics">
    Check the boxes next to every topic you want to include in the paper. Each topic shows the number of available questions in the bank, so you can plan coverage confidently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and print">
    Click **Generate Preview** to see the composed question paper — complete with section headers and answer-space formatting. When you are satisfied, click **Print Question Paper** to send it to your printer or save it as a PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your account has the **`schedule_planner:can_plan_others`** permission, you can plan and edit sessions on behalf of other faculty members. A faculty selector dropdown will appear at the top of the planner, letting you switch between users. Administrators typically hold this permission and use it to manage department-wide timetables from a central location. If you believe you should have this access but do not, contact your institution's Chaarvy administrator.
</Note>
