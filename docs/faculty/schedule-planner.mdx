The Schedule Planner is a calendar where you assign syllabus topics to specific dates and periods. You can use it to plan upcoming classes, reschedule topics, and track which topics are completed, pending, or overdue.

Go to **Faculty Corner** > **Schedule Planner** to open it.

## Understand the planner

### Coverage progress

The **Coverage Progress** bar at the top of the page shows the percentage of planned topics that are completed, for example `60% (6/10)`. Hover over the bar to see the **Completed**, **Pending**, and **Overdue** counts.

The counts cover the topics planned in the date range currently shown on the calendar. A topic is overdue when its date has passed and it is not marked complete.

### Calendar views

Use the **Week** and **Month** toggle to switch views. The planner opens in **Week** view.

- Click the left and right arrows to move to the previous or next week or month.
- Click **Today** to return to the current date.

**Week** view shows a grid of days and period slots. **Month** view shows each day as a single cell with its planned topics listed.

### What the calendar shows

| Item | What it means |
|---|---|
| Blue topic block | A planned topic that is pending. |
| Green topic block with a check mark | A topic marked complete. |
| Red topic block | An overdue topic. Its date has passed and it is not complete. |
| **Weekend** label | Sunday. You can't schedule topics on Sundays. |
| Holiday name | A holiday from **Administration** > **Holidays**. You can't schedule topics on holidays. |
| **Need a topic** | In **Week** view, a period that has a class in your timetable but no topic planned. Hover over it to see the program, segment, medium, and subject from your timetable. |
| **Need topic** count | In **Month** view, the number of timetable classes on that day with no topic planned. |

Hover over a topic block to see the topic name and subject.

## Plan a topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the planning panel">
    Click **+ Plan Schedule**, or click an empty period (in **Week** view) or a day (in **Month** view). The **Plan New Schedule** panel opens on the right. You can't click past dates or break periods to plan a topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Complete all of the following fields. Each one is required.

    | Field | Details |
    |---|---|
    | **Date** | Past dates, Sundays, and holidays can't be selected. |
    | **Period** | Shows each period slot with its start and end time. Breaks aren't listed. |
    | **Program** | The program for the class. |
    | **Segment** | Available after you select a program. |
    | **Subject** | Lists subjects for the selected program and segment. |
    | **Medium** | Lists mediums for the selected program and segment. |
    | **Section** | Available after you select a medium. |
    | **Topic** | Available after you select a subject. Type to search by topic name. Each topic shows its question count, if it has questions. |

    If you opened the panel from a period that has a class in your timetable, the panel shows **Pre-filled from your Timetable (Editable)**. The program, segment, subject, medium, and section are filled in for you. You can change any of them. You always select the topic yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Schedule**. The button stays disabled until all fields are filled in. The topic then appears on the calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Topics come from **Faculty Corner** > **Topics Management**. If the topic list is empty, add topics for the subject first. See [Topics and question bank](/academics/topics-question-bank).
</Note>

## Update a planned topic

Click a topic block on the calendar to open the **Schedule Details** panel. The panel shows the topic name with its program, segment, section, and subject.

You can make these changes:

- **Date**: Pick a new date. Past dates, Sundays, and holidays can't be selected.
- **Period**: Pick a different period slot.
- **Topic Status**: Click **Mark Complete** to mark the topic as completed. Click **Mark Pending** to undo it.

Each change saves as soon as you make it. Click **Done** to close the panel.

To remove the topic from the calendar, click **Delete Schedule**.

<Warning>
  **Delete Schedule** removes the topic right away. There is no confirmation step.
</Warning>

## Plan for other faculty members

The user selector next to **+ Plan Schedule** shows whose schedule you are viewing. By default, it shows your own schedule.

If your role has the `schedule_planner:can_plan_others` permission, you can select another user from this list. The calendar, coverage, and timetable details then switch to that user, and any topics you plan are saved to their schedule. Without this permission, the selector is disabled.
