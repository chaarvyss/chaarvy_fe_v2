Chaarvy includes an internal chat called **Campus Chat**. You can message other users directly, chat in groups, and send broadcasts.

## Open chat

Click the message icon in the top bar. Its tooltip reads **Internal Chat**. A red badge on the icon shows your total number of unread messages.

The chat page lists your conversations on the left, under **Direct Messages** and **Groups & Channels**. To find a conversation, type in **Search conversations...**. Conversations with unread messages show a count on the avatar.

For direct chats, a green dot on the avatar means the person is online.

## Start a conversation

The buttons at the top of **Campus Chat** depend on your role permissions. You may see some, all, or none of them.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Direct chat">
    1. Click the **+** icon (**Start Chat**). The **Start Direct Chat** dialog opens.
    2. Search by name or category, or open the advanced filters to narrow the list.
    3. Click a contact to open the conversation.

    If no one appears, the dialog shows **No contacts available based on your class/section and communication permissions.**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Group">
    1. Click the **Create Group** icon. The **Create Chat Group** dialog opens.
    2. Enter a **Group Title**. You can also add a **Description (Optional)**.
    3. Search for members and select them from the list.
    4. Click **Create Group**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Broadcast">
    1. Click the **Create Broadcast** icon. The **Send Broadcast** dialog opens.
    2. Enter a **Broadcast Title** and a **Broadcast Message**.
    3. Search for recipients and select them from the list.
    4. Click **Send Broadcast**. The button shows the number of selected recipients.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Filter contacts

In the direct chat, group, and broadcast dialogs, click the filter toggle (**Toggle Advanced Filters**) to filter contacts by **Program**, **Segment**, **Medium**, **Section**, and **Admission Number**. Click **Clear Filters** to remove all filters.

## Send messages

1. Type in **Type a message...**.
2. Press **Enter** or click the send icon.

You can also:

- Click the emoji icon to insert an emoji.
- Click the paperclip icon to attach a file. You can attach images, videos, PDF files, and Word documents (.doc and .docx).

Status icons on your own messages show whether a message is sending, sent, delivered, or read. A blue double check means the message was read. If a message fails to send, click the alert icon next to it to resend.

## Message actions

Click the three dot icon on a message to open its menu:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Reply** | Replies to the message. The reply is labeled **Replied**. |
| **Edit** | Edits the message. Available only for your own messages. Edited messages show **(edited)**. |
| **Forward** | Opens **Forward Message To...** so you can pick a conversation. Forwarded messages are labeled **Forwarded**. |
| **Delete for me** | Hides the message from your view only. |
| **Delete for everyone** | Deletes the message for all participants. Available only for your own messages. The text changes to "This message was deleted". |

Chaarvy asks you to confirm before it deletes a message.

To delete several messages at once, click the **Select Messages** icon in the conversation header. Select the messages, then click the delete icon.

## Manage group members

1. In a group conversation, click the avatar in the header to see the participant list.
2. To add people, click **Add**. In **Add Members**, select contacts and click **Add**.
3. To let new members see earlier messages, turn on **Share chat history**.
4. To remove someone, click the **Remove Member** icon next to their name and confirm.
