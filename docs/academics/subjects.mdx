Subjects sit at the heart of your academic calendar in Chaarvy. Before you can build a timetable, record attendance, or organise teaching topics, each subject must exist in the system and be connected to the correct program segment and teaching faculty. This page walks you through the full lifecycle of a subject.

## Creating a Subject

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subjects module">
    From the Admin sidebar, navigate to **Subjects**. The list shows all subjects currently configured in your institution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Subject">
    Select the **Add Subject** button to open the subject creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter subject details">
    Fill in the required fields:
    - **Subject Name** — the full name as it should appear in timetables and reports (for example, *Mathematical Analysis* or *Organisational Behaviour*).
    - **Subject Code** — a short identifier used in timetable slots and transcripts (for example, *MA201*).
    - Any additional fields your institution has configured, such as credit hours or subject type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the subject">
    Click **Save**. The subject is now available to be assigned to program segments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Subjects to Program Segments

Once your subjects are created, you map them to program segments using the **Subject Assignment** tool. This tells Chaarvy which subjects belong to which programs and segments, making them available for timetable building and attendance tracking.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subject Assignment">
    From the Subjects list, click the **Assign Subjects** button. The Subject Assignment panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select programs and subjects">
    Use the **Select Programs** and **Select Subjects** filters to choose the programs and subjects you want to work with. The assignment matrix below updates to show the relevant combinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark assignments in the matrix">
    The matrix displays programs and their segments as rows, with subjects as columns. Check the box at each intersection where a subject should be taught for a given program segment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the assignments">
    Click **Save** to apply all changes. The selected subjects are now linked to the chosen program segments and will appear when building those segments' timetables.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Subject assignments are used by other Chaarvy modules automatically. When you build a **Timetable**, only subjects that have been assigned to the selected program segment are available to place in time slots. When faculty open the **Attendance Register**, they see only the classes for subjects assigned to them — so keeping assignments accurate is essential for both scheduling and daily attendance tracking.
</Note>
