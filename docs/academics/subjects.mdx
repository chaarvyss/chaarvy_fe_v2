Subjects must exist in Chaarvy before you can schedule them in a timetable or create topics for them. After you create a subject, assign it to the program segments where it's taught.

In the app, subjects live under the **Administration** menu. Go to **Administration** > **Subjects** to open the subjects list.

## The subjects list

The table shows these columns:

- **S#**
- **Subject Name**
- **Status**: shows **Active** or **Inactive**. Click the chip to switch it.
- **Actions**: a pencil icon to edit the subject.

By default, the list shows only active subjects.

- To search, type in the **Search...** box.
- To filter by status, click the filter icon. In the **Filters** panel, choose **Active** or **Inactive**, then click **Search**. Click **Reset** to clear the filter.

## Add a subject

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Subject panel">
    Click **Add Subject**. A side panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name">
    Type the name in the **Subject Name** field. This field is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To rename a subject, click the pencil icon in its row. The **Update Subject** panel opens. Change the **Subject Name** and click **Submit**.

## Assign subjects to program segments

The **Subject Assignment** matrix links subjects to the program segments where they're taught.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Subject Assignment">
    Click **Assign Subjects**. The **Subject Assignment** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose programs and subjects">
    Use **Select Programs** and **Select Subjects** to pick what appears in the matrix. Only active subjects are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark assignments">
    Each row is a subject. Columns are grouped by program, with one column per segment. Select the checkbox where a subject is taught in that segment. Changed cells are highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the save icon above the matrix. Its tooltip reads **Save changes** when you have unsaved edits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Shortcuts in the matrix

- Select the checkbox next to a segment name to assign every listed subject to that segment.
- Select the checkbox next to a subject name to assign that subject to every listed segment.
- Select the checkbox in the **Subjects** header to select every cell.
- Click a segment name to copy its assignments. Choose the target segments, then click **Apply and Continue**.

## Where subject assignments are used

- **Topics Management**: only subjects assigned to the selected program and segment appear in the **Subject** list. See [Topics and question bank](/academics/topics-question-bank).
- **Time Table**: the subject picker lists subjects for the selected class. See [Timetable](/academics/timetable).
