The attendance register is where faculty mark attendance for a class period. Each student appears as a photo card that you click to mark present or absent.

Go to **Faculty Corner** > **Attendence Register** to open it.

## How the register loads your class

When you open the register, Chaarvy checks your timetable for the class you're teaching now. If it finds one, it fills in the **Program**, **Segment**, **Medium**, and **Section** for you and uses the current period.

If you open a record from the attendance log, the register loads that record instead, including its date, period, and saved statuses. See [Attendance log](/faculty/attendance-log).

The header shows the selected date and the **Period**. Click the date to pick a different day.

## Mark attendance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the register">
    Go to **Faculty Corner** > **Attendence Register**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the class">
    Confirm the **Program**, **Segment**, **Medium**, and **Section**. Change any of them if needed. Changing a field clears the fields after it. Only active programs are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark each student">
    The **Students List** shows a card for each student in the class. Every student starts as pending. Click a card once to mark the student present (green). Click again to mark them absent (red). Each further click switches between present and absent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    The bar above the cards shows **Total**, **Present**, **Absent**, and **Pending** counts. Keep going until **Pending** reaches 0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The **Last saved at** time appears below the buttons. You can save as often as you like before you finalize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize">
    Click **Finalize** to lock the record. Chaarvy then takes you to the attendance log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no students match the selected class, the page shows **No students found for the selected criteria.**

## Rules for saving and finalizing

- **Save** and **Finalize** stay disabled until you select all four fields.
- Saving needs a period. The register takes it from your timetable or from the log record you opened.
- **Finalize** is only enabled for a saved record, and only for the faculty member who recorded it.
- After you finalize, the cards are locked and both buttons are disabled.

<Warning>
  You can't edit a finalized record from the register. Check every student's status before you click **Finalize**.
</Warning>

## Review past attendance

Saved and finalized records appear under **Faculty Corner** > **Attendence**. In that log, draft records show the **Draft** status and finalized records show **Saved**. Click a row to reopen it in the register. See [Attendance log](/faculty/attendance-log).
