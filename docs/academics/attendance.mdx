Chaarvy's attendance module gives faculty a fast, structured way to record who is present for every class period. Once attendance is taken, administrators and faculty can review historical logs to monitor student participation and identify patterns early. This page explains both sides of the process — recording attendance and reviewing it afterwards.

## Taking Attendance

Faculty members take attendance from the **Attendance Register**. Select the correct class using the on-screen filters, then mark each student before finalizing the record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Attendance Register">
    Log in as a faculty member and navigate to **Faculty → Attendance Register** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the class">
    Use the **Program**, **Segment**, **Medium**, and **Section** dropdowns to choose the class you are taking attendance for. Chaarvy loads the full list of enrolled students once all four selections are made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the date and period">
    Check the date and period shown at the top of the register. You can adjust the date using the date picker if you are recording attendance for a previous session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark each student present or absent">
    Tap or click each student's card to toggle their attendance status. A green card indicates present; a red card indicates absent. All students start as unmarked — work through the list until every student has a status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your progress">
    Click **Save** at any point to preserve your work. You can save and return to the register later to continue marking before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the completion progress">
    A progress indicator shows the percentage of students whose attendance has been recorded. Make sure it reaches 100% before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize attendance">
    Once all students are marked, click **Finalize**. This locks the register for that class period and submits the records to the system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Once attendance is **finalized**, it cannot be edited. Double-check every student's status before clicking Finalize. If a correction is genuinely needed after finalization, contact your institution's administrator to handle the amendment.
</Note>

## Viewing Attendance Logs

The Attendance Log gives faculty and administrators a historical view of attendance records across any date range or class. Use it to monitor individual student participation, prepare reports, or investigate unexplained absences.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Attendance Log">
    Navigate to **Faculty → Attendance Log** in the sidebar. Administrators can access the same log from the Admin section with broader filter options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Use the available filters to narrow down the records you want to see:
    - **Date or date range** — select a single day or a span of dates.
    - **Program, Segment, Medium, Section** — drill down to a specific class.
    - **Subject** — view attendance for one subject at a time if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the records">
    The log displays each class session matching your filters, along with a breakdown of present and absent counts. Click on any session to see the individual student-level attendance for that class period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export if needed">
    If your institution has export enabled, use the **Export** button to download the filtered attendance data as a spreadsheet for offline reporting or parent communication.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The Attendance Log only shows records for classes where attendance has been finalized. Sessions that were started but not finalized do not appear in the log, which is another reason to always complete the finalization step after marking attendance.
</Info>
