**Topics Management** helps faculty break each subject into topics and build a question bank for every topic. Questions are grouped by mark value and can be written in each medium the class is taught in.

Go to **Faculty Corner** > **Topics Management** to open it.

## Create a topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the subject">
    Select a **Program**, a **Class**, and a **Subject**. The **Class** list shows the program's segments. The **Subject** list shows the subjects assigned to that program segment. See [Subjects](/academics/subjects).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the topic form">
    Click **+ Add New Topic**. The button is enabled once you select all three fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Type a **Topic Title**. You can also add a **Description (Optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Topic**. The button is disabled until you enter a title.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your topics appear under **Configured Topics** as cards. Each card shows the title, the description, and the number of questions, for example **12 Qs**. If the subject has no topics yet, you see **No topics configured yet**.

To edit a topic, click the pencil icon on its card. Update the fields in the **Edit Topic** dialog, then click **Update Topic**.

## Open a question bank

Click a topic card, or its **Manage Question Bank →** link. The page changes to *topic name* **- Question Bank**. Click **← Back** to return to your topics.

## Add a mark group

A mark group holds questions of one question type. Every question in the group carries the same marks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the group form">
    Click **+ Add New Mark Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the question type">
    Select a **Question Type**. The **Marks Each Question** field fills in automatically for that type and can't be edited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Group**. The group appears as an expandable section labeled with its type and **Marks Each**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each question type can be used once per topic. If you pick a type that already has a group, Chaarvy shows **Mark group already exists**.

## Add questions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand the mark group">
    Click the mark group to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the question format">
    Click **+ Add MCQ** for a multiple-choice question or **+ Add Theory** for a written-answer question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the question">
    Expand the new question. Under **Question Title**, fill in the **Question Text** field for each medium.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add options for an MCQ">
    For an MCQ, fill in **Option 1** to **Option 4** for each medium. Then pick the **Correct Answer** for each medium.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Question** inside the question, or **Save** in its header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each question header shows its number, a preview of the text, its format (**MCQ** or **THEORY**), and a **Draft** or **Saved** tag. Unsaved questions are marked **Draft**.

### Validation

Chaarvy checks each question before saving:

- The question needs question text in at least one medium.
- An MCQ needs all 4 options filled in English.
- An MCQ needs a correct answer selected in English.

### Translate a question

If the class has more than one medium, click **🌍 Auto-Translate** in the question header. Chaarvy translates the English question text, and the English MCQ options, into the other mediums. The button is disabled until the English question text is filled in. Review the translations, then save the question.

## Edit or delete

- **Edit a mark group**: click the pencil icon on the group header. Choose a new **Question Type** and click **Update Group**.
- **Delete a mark group**: click the trash icon on the group header, then click **Delete** to confirm. This deletes the group and all its questions.
- **Edit a question**: expand it, make your changes, then click **Update Question** or **Update**.
- **Delete a question**: click **Remove** in the question header, then click **Delete** to confirm.

<Warning>
  Deleting a mark group or question can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Where topics are used

Topics you create here are available to choose when you plan classes in the [Schedule planner](/faculty/schedule-planner).
