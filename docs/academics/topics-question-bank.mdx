The Topics and Question Bank module in Chaarvy helps faculty keep their teaching content organised and their exam preparation efficient. You create topics to structure what you teach within each subject, and then build a bank of questions under each topic that can be drawn on when generating question papers through the Schedule Planner. This page explains how to set up topics and populate the question bank.

## Creating Topics

Topics break a subject down into teachable units — similar to chapters or modules. Creating them in Chaarvy before adding questions keeps your question bank well-organised and makes it easy to pull questions on a specific area during exam planning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Topics module">
    Log in as a faculty member and navigate to **Faculty → Topics** in the sidebar. You will see a list of all topics you have already created, grouped by subject.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the program, segment, and subject">
    Use the filters at the top of the page to choose the **Program**, **Segment**, and **Subject** for which you want to create a topic. The topic list updates to show existing topics for that combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Topic">
    Select the **Add Topic** button to open the topic creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter topic details">
    Fill in the following fields:
    - **Topic Name** — a concise, descriptive title (for example, *Demand and Supply Analysis* or *Introduction to Recursion*).
    - **Description** — a brief summary of what the topic covers. This helps colleagues and administrators understand the scope at a glance.
    - Any additional fields configured by your institution, such as teaching hours or learning outcomes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the topic">
    Click **Save**. The topic now appears under the selected subject and is ready for questions to be added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Building the Question Bank

Each topic has its own question bank, organised into **mark groups**. A mark group bundles questions that carry the same number of marks — for example, all 1-mark questions in one group and all 5-mark questions in another. This structure lets you quickly assemble a balanced question paper during exam planning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a topic's question bank">
    From the Topics list, find the topic you want to populate and click **Question Bank** next to it. The question bank view opens, showing any mark groups and questions already added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a mark group">
    Click **+ Add New Mark Group**, select the question type (which determines the marks per question), and click **Save Group**. The new group appears as a collapsible section in the question bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions to a mark group">
    Expand the mark group, then click **+ Add MCQ** to add a multiple-choice question or **+ Add Theory** to add a written-answer question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the question content">
    For MCQ questions, type the question stem, add all answer options, and mark the correct one. For theory questions, enter the question text and, optionally, a model answer or key points.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the question">
    Click **Save** on the question card. The question is added to the mark group and is immediately available for exam paper generation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for all questions">
    Continue adding questions across mark groups until the topic's bank is complete. A richer bank gives you more flexibility when customising question papers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Managing Mark Groups and Questions

You can edit or remove mark groups and individual questions at any time using the action icons on each card:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Edit a mark group" icon="pen-to-square">
    Click the edit icon on a mark group header to change its question type or marks value. This is useful if you need to reorganise your bank after a curriculum revision.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delete a question or group" icon="trash">
    Click the delete icon on any question or mark group header to remove it. Deleting a mark group removes all questions within it, so confirm before proceeding.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The question bank you build here feeds directly into the **Schedule Planner** module, where Chaarvy can automatically assemble a question paper by drawing from this bank based on topic, question type, and mark group. The more comprehensive your question bank, the more useful the auto-generation feature becomes — aim to add at least ten questions per topic before your first exam cycle.
</Tip>
