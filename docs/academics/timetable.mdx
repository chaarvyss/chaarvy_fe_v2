The **Time Table** page lets you define the daily period structure once, then build a weekly timetable for each class. A class is a combination of program, segment, medium, and section.

Go to **Academics** > **Time Table** to open it.

## Before you start

Make sure these are in place:

- The program is **Active**. See [Programs](/academics/programs).
- The segment has at least one medium and one section with a seating capacity greater than 0. You set these on the program's **Mediums** and **Sections (capacity)** tabs.
- Subjects are assigned to the program segment. See [Subjects](/academics/subjects).

## Define the period structure

The period structure sets the time slots used by every timetable. Chaarvy uses a single structure for your institution.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the builder">
    On the **Time Table** page, click **Template designer**. The **Timetable Structure Builder** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the day boundaries">
    Set the **Day Start Time** and **Day End Time**. Enter a **Default Duration** in minutes for class periods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate or add slots">
    If you haven't saved a structure yet, click **Auto-Generate Slots** to fill the day with periods of the default duration. You can also click **Add class period** or **Add break** to add slots one at a time. A new break defaults to 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust each slot">
    Each row has a **Slot Name**, **Start Time**, **Duration**, and **End Time**. Use the **Type** switch to mark a slot as a break or a class. Click the delete icon to remove a slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Schedule Template**. The button is disabled while any slot has a duration of 0 or less.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Slots are chained. Only the first slot's start time is editable. When you change a slot's duration or end time, every later slot shifts automatically. Changing **Default Duration** updates all class periods but leaves breaks unchanged.
</Tip>

The builder also shows the **Total Operating Window**, the time the current slots end, and a count of total slots, classes, and breaks.

## Build a class timetable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a program">
    On the **Time Table** page, click a program card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a segment">
    Choose a segment from **Available Segments**. On smaller screens, use the **Select Segment** dropdown. The **Timetable Scheduler** opens for that segment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the section and medium">
    Use the section buttons and medium buttons at the top of the scheduler. The first section and medium are selected by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a period">
    Click a cell in the grid. Rows are days of the week and columns are the periods from your period structure. Break columns can't be selected. In the popup, choose a **Subject**, then choose a **Faculty** member. The cell updates when you pick the faculty member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear a period">
    To remove an assignment, click the cell and then click **Clear Slot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** at the top of the scheduler.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Assignments are not saved until you click **Save**. Save before you switch to another section or medium, or close the scheduler.
</Warning>

## Faculty availability

The **Faculty** list only shows faculty members who can teach the selected subject and are free in that period. Faculty you have already placed in the same period on the current grid are also excluded. If nobody is free, the field label changes to **No faculty available**.

## View a faculty member's timetable

While choosing a faculty member for a period, click the info icon next to a name in the **Faculty** list. Its tooltip reads **View Faculty Timetable**. The **Faculty Timetable** dialog shows that person's weekly schedule. Each entry shows the subject, medium, segment, and section.

To leave the scheduler, close it. Click **Back to Programs** to choose a different program.
