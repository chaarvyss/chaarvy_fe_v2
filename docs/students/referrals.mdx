The Referrals feature helps your institution understand which channels and individuals are driving student enrollments. By tracking referral data in one place, you can evaluate the effectiveness of your outreach efforts, recognise high-performing referral sources, and make more informed decisions about where to focus recruitment activities.

## Viewing the Referrals Summary

Navigate to **Admin → Referrals** to open the referral summary dashboard. The table loads automatically and displays a summary row for each referrer your institution has recorded.

### What the Dashboard Shows

The summary table includes the following columns for each referrer:

- **Referee Name** — the name of the person or source that referred students to your institution.
- **Total Admissions** — the number of students attributed to that referrer.
- **Committed Admission Fees** — the total fee amount committed by students referred through that source.

Summary cards at the top of the page show the combined totals across all referrers — overall admissions referred and total fees committed — giving you a quick institution-wide overview.

### Filtering Referral Data

Use the filter icon to open the filter panel, where you can narrow the table by:

- **Referred By** — search for a specific referee by name.
- **Date Range** — limit results to a particular enrollment period.

<Info>
  Referral data is recorded at the point of admission. Make sure staff filling in the admission form capture the referral source accurately so that the dashboard reflects a true picture of your enrollment channels.
</Info>

---

<Note>
  Access to the Referrals dashboard requires the **referrels:view:list** permission. If you do not see the Referrals option under the Admin menu, ask your system administrator to grant you this permission.
</Note>
