The referrals page summarizes admissions by the person who referred each student. The referrer is the user selected in the **Referred by** field of the [admission form](/students/admission-form#student-base-details).

To open it, go to **Student Management** > **Referrels**. The page title is **Admission referral summary**.

## Summary cards

- **Total Admissions**: the total number of admissions across all referrers in the table.
- **Total Fees**: the total committed admission fees across all referrers in the table.

## Referral table

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **S#** | Row number. |
| **Referee Name** | The user who referred the students. |
| **Total admissions** | The number of admissions referred by this user. |
| **Committed Admission fees** | The total admission fees committed by the students this user referred. |

## Filter referrals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filters">
    Click the filter icon in the table header. The **Filters** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your filters">
    - **Referred by**: choose a referrer.
    - **Date range**: click **Select date range** and pick a start and end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filters">
    Click **Search** to apply the filters. Click **Reset** to clear them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The summary cards update to match the filtered results.
