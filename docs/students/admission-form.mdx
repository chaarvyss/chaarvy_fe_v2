The admission form guides you through everything needed to enroll a new student in your institution. It is organised into five tabs — from basic identification all the way through to fees — so that no important detail is missed. Once you save a step, you can freely navigate between completed tabs to review or correct information before final submission.

<Warning>
  Complete all required fields on each step before clicking **Next**. The form will not advance if mandatory information is missing, and unsaved data on the current step may be lost if you navigate away from the page entirely.
</Warning>

## Completing the Admission Form

Navigate to **Student Management → Admissions** and click **New Admission** to open the form. Work through each tab in order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Student Base Details">
    Set up the student's core identity and program placement.

    - **Student Name** — enter the student's full legal name as it should appear on official documents.
    - **Photo Upload** — upload a recent passport-size photograph of the student. Accepted formats are JPG and PNG.
    - **Program** — select the degree, diploma, or certificate program the student is enrolling in.
    - **Segment** — choose the academic segment or year level within the program.
    - **Medium** — select the language medium of instruction for this student.
    - **Section** — assign the student to a class section.

    Once all required fields are filled, click **Next** to proceed to student details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Student Details">
    Capture the student's demographic and academic background information.

    - **Date of Birth** — enter the student's date of birth. This is used for age verification and official records.
    - **Gender** — select the student's gender.
    - **Blood Group** — record the student's blood group for health and emergency purposes.
    - **Religion** — select the student's religion if required for institutional or government reporting.
    - **Community** — specify the student's community category (for example, General, OBC, SC, ST) as relevant to your institution's requirements.
    - **Qualified Exam** — record the last qualifying examination passed by the student, including marks or grade where applicable.
    - **Occupation Information** — capture details about the student's or guardian's occupation if required for financial aid or records.

    Click **Next** when all required fields are complete.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Student Address">
    Record both the student's permanent home address and the address where they currently reside.

    - **Permanent Address** — the student's long-term home address. Fill in the street, city, state, pin code, and country.
    - **Correspondence Address** — the address to use for all official communication. If it is the same as the permanent address, use the **Copy from Permanent Address** option to auto-fill the fields.

    Accurate address information is important for sending admission letters, fee notices, and other official correspondence.

    Click **Next** to move on to add-on courses.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add-On Courses">
    Enroll the student in any optional courses offered alongside their primary program.

    Browse the list of available add-on courses and select all that apply to this student. Each add-on course you select will be reflected in the fees breakdown on the next step.

    If the student is not enrolling in any add-on courses, leave all options unselected and click **Next** to continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fees Details">
    Review the complete fee breakdown and set the payable amount for this admission.

    The fees panel automatically calculates and displays:

    - **Course Fees** — the standard tuition fee for the selected program.
    - **Book Fees** — the cost of study materials associated with the program.
    - **Add-On Course Fees** — fees for any add-on courses selected in the previous step.
    - **Total Payable Amount** — the combined total. You can adjust the payable amount if a discount, scholarship, or instalment arrangement applies.

    Confirm the fee details and click **Submit** to complete the admission record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The form saves your progress on each step as you advance. If you need to pause mid-way, you can navigate back to a completed tab to review or correct information without losing data already entered on later steps.
</Tip>

## Application Fees

After you submit the admission form, a processing fee modal may appear depending on your institution's configuration. This step collects the application processing fee before the admission is finalised.

- Review the processing fee amount shown in the modal.
- Select the payment method and enter the relevant transaction details.
- Click **Confirm Payment** to record the fee and complete the enrollment.

Once the application fee is paid, the student's admission record becomes active and appears in the **Admissions** list.

<Note>
  Creating a new admission requires the **admissions:create_admission** permission. If you do not see the **New Admission** button, contact your system administrator to request access.
</Note>
