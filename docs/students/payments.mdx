Use **Collect Payment** to record fee payments against a student's enrollment and print receipts.

To open it, go to **Student Management** > **Collect Payment**. You can also click **Collect Payment** on the **Accounts** > **Payments** page. See [Payments](/accounts/payments).

## Find the student

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search by admission number">
    Enter the student's admission number in the **Admission Number** field and click **Search**. If no student matches, the message **Details not found** appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the student's fees">
    The page shows the student's **Admission Number**, **Father Name**, and **Student Name**. Below that, a table lists each course enrollment with these columns:

    | Column | Description |
    |--------|-------------|
    | **Course Name** | The program. |
    | **Segment** | The class within the program. |
    | **Medium** | The medium of instruction. |
    | **Section** | The assigned section. |
    | **Total** | The total payable fee. |
    | **Paid** | The amount paid so far. Shown in green when greater than zero. |
    | **Pending** | The amount still due. Shown in red when greater than zero. |
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The **Total** comes from the fees finalized on the **Fees Details** tab of the [admission form](/students/admission-form#fees-details).
</Tip>

## Record a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Collect Fees window">
    Click the **Pending** amount for the enrollment. The **Collect Fees** window opens and shows the **Pending Fees**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the payment details">
    - **Amount**: the amount being paid now. It must be greater than zero and can't exceed the pending fees, so you can record a partial payment.
    - **Payment Method**: select **Cash**.
    - **Payment date**: defaults to today. Change it if needed.
    - **Notes**: optional. Keep notes to 500 characters or fewer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the payment">
    Click **Collect Payment**. The button stays disabled until you enter a valid amount and select a payment method.

    After the payment is saved, Chaarvy shows **Payment recorded successfully**, generates the receipt, and opens your browser's print dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View payment history and reprint receipts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment history">
    Click the **Paid** amount for the enrollment. The **Payment History** window opens for that admission number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review past payments">
    The table lists each payment with **Particulars**, **Paid on**, **Amount**, **Mode**, and **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reprint a receipt">
    Click the download icon (**Download reciept**) on a row. The receipt opens in your browser's print dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the receipt can't be generated, the message **Failed to generate reciept** appears.
