The Payments module brings together everything your finance and administrative staff need to manage student fee collection. You can review a complete history of transactions, identify students with outstanding balances, accept payments at the counter, and generate official receipts — without switching between separate tools.

## Viewing Payment History

Navigate to **Admin → Payments** to access the payments list. The table shows all recorded fee transactions with the student's admission number, name, payment status, amount, and payment date displayed for each row.

### Filtering Transactions

Use the filter icon to open the filter panel. You can narrow results by:

- **Search** — find a transaction by student name or admission number
- **Date Range** — limit results to a specific time period
- **Status** — show only successful or failed transactions

### Printing a Receipt from History

To reprint a receipt for a past transaction, locate the row in the payments list and click the receipt icon in the **Action** column. The receipt will open ready to print. This is useful when a student requests a duplicate receipt for a payment they made previously.

## Collecting Payments

To collect a fee payment from a student, use the **Collect Payment** workflow. You can reach it by clicking the **Collect Payment** button on the payments list, or by navigating directly to **Student Management → Payments → Pending Payments**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the Student">
    Enter the student's **Admission Number** in the search field and click **Search**. The page will display the student's details — name, father's name, and admission number — along with a breakdown of their fee records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Fee Breakdown">
    The fee table shows each enrolled course along with the total amount due, the amount already paid, and the amount still pending. Review the outstanding balance with the student before proceeding.

    Click the **Pending** amount for the relevant course to open the payment collection panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Payment Details">
    In the **Collect Fees** panel, fill in the following:

    - **Amount** — the amount the student is paying now. This can be the full balance or a partial instalment if your institution allows instalments.
    - **Payment Method** — select the method used (for example, Cash).
    - **Payment Date** — confirm or adjust the date of the transaction.
    - **Notes** — add any optional notes for your records.

    Click **Collect Payment** to record the transaction. A receipt will be generated automatically once the payment is saved.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Print the Receipt">
    After the payment is recorded, the receipt opens automatically for printing. Hand a copy to the student and retain one for your records if needed.

    You can also reprint the receipt at any time from the **Admin → Payments** list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Collecting payments requires specific permissions. Make sure your role includes:

  - **collect_payment:search_pending_payment** — to search for and view pending balances
  - **collect_payment:accept_payment** — to record a new payment transaction
  - **collect_payment:print_receipt** — to print a receipt after payment

  Contact your system administrator if any of these permissions are missing from your account.
</Note>

## Payment Receipts

A payment receipt is generated automatically as soon as a transaction is recorded. Receipts can be printed from two places:

- **During payment collection** — the receipt opens immediately after a payment is confirmed.
- **From payment history** — click the receipt icon on any row in the **Admin → Payments** list to reprint it at any time.

Each receipt includes the student's name and admission number, the program they are enrolled in, a line-by-line fee breakdown, the amount paid, the payment method, and the date of the transaction.

<Tip>
  If a student requests a duplicate receipt for a past payment, locate the original transaction in the **Admin → Payments** list and click the receipt icon in the Action column to reprint it.
</Tip>
