The **College Profile** page holds your institution's name, contact details, registration numbers, address, and logo.

Go to **Administration** > **College Profile**.

The left panel shows your logo and a summary: **College Code**, **College Name**, **Campus Name**, and **Contact Number**. The right side has two tabs: **College Details** and **Address**.

## Update college details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the College Details tab">
    Select the **College Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the fields">
    You can edit these fields:
    - **COLLEGE NAME**
    - **CAMPUS NAME**
    - **CONTACT NUMBERS**
    - **UDISE NUMBER**
    - **JANANABHUMI NUMBER**

    **COLLEGE CODE** is read-only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    When you change a value, an **Update Profile** button appears. Click it to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update the address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Address tab">
    Select the **Address** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the address">
    Fill in these fields:
    - **Door No** (required)
    - **House / Apartment** (required)
    - **Street** (required)
    - **Landmark**
    - **Village / City** (required)
    - **State** (required)
    - **District** (required)
    - **Pincode** (required, exactly 6 digits)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the logo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an image">
    Click the pencil icon on the logo and select a PNG or JPEG image. A preview appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the logo">
    Click the save icon that appears next to the logo.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your logo and campus name appear at the top of the sidebar.
