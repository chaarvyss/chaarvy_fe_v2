Your College Profile is the single source of truth for your institution's identity in Chaarvy. The details you enter here — your institution's name, contact information, address, and logo — appear across the platform and on documents issued to students, such as admission letters and fee receipts. Keeping this information accurate and up to date reflects well on your institution.

## Accessing the College Profile

Navigate to **College Profile** from the main sidebar. This page is separate from the Admin section and is accessible directly from the top-level navigation.

## Institution Details

The **Details** section holds your institution's core information.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Details section">
    On the College Profile page, locate the **Institution Details** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update institution information">
    Click **Edit** to modify any of the following fields:
    - **Institution Name** — the official name of your college or university.
    - **Contact Number** — the primary phone number for your institution.
    - **Description** — a brief overview of your institution that may appear on public-facing materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the updates. Changes take effect immediately across the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Address

Keep your institution's physical address current so it appears correctly on official documents.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Address section">
    Scroll to the **Address** panel on the College Profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the address">
    Click **Edit** and fill in your institution's full postal address, including street, city, state, and pincode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to confirm the updated address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## College Logo

Your institution's logo is used on admission letters, fee receipts, and other student-facing documents generated by Chaarvy.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Logo section">
    Locate the **Logo** panel at the top of the College Profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload or replace the logo">
    Click the logo area or the **Upload Logo** button to select an image file from your device. For best results, use a PNG file with a transparent background and a minimum resolution of 200×200 pixels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upload">
    Once uploaded, the new logo is saved automatically and will appear on all newly generated documents going forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The College Profile uses separate permissions for viewing and editing. You need `college_profile:details:view` to see institution details and `college_profile:details:edit` to modify them. The same pattern applies to the address (`college_profile:address:view` / `college_profile:address:edit`) and logo (`college_profile:icon:view` / `college_profile:icon:update`). If you can view but not edit any section, contact your system administrator to request edit permissions.
</Note>
