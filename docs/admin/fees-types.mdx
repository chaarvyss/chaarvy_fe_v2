Fee types are the categories your institution uses to classify charges, such as tuition, exam, or lab fees. You use them when you set up fees for a program.

Go to **Administration** > **Fees types**. The **Fees Types** page shows an editable table with a **#** column and a **Fees type** column.

## How editing works

You make all changes in the table first. Nothing is saved until you click **Submit**.

- Rows are color-coded as **New Rows**, **Updated Rows**, and **Pending Deletions**.
- Use the **Undo** and **Redo** icons to step back or forward through your edits.
- Click **Reset** to discard all unsaved changes.

## Add a fee type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a row">
    Click **Add New Row** below the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name">
    Type the fee type name in the new row, for example *Tuition Fee* or *Exam Fee*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename a fee type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the row">
    Click the pencil icon on the row and change the name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the row">
    Click the check icon to finish editing the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a fee type

Click the delete icon on the row, then click **Submit**. The fee type is marked inactive.

<Warning>
  Check whether a fee type is used in any program fee structure before you remove it.
</Warning>

## Review the results

After you click **Submit**, a **Process Summary** window shows the progress of your changes. It lists counts for **Creating**, **Updating**, **Skipped**, **Failed**, and **Exceptions**.

<Tip>
  After you create fee types, set up program fees on the [Programs](/academics/programs) page.
</Tip>
