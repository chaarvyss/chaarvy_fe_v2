The **Reports** page lets you download admission reports and Excel templates for bulk uploads.

Go to **Administration** > **Reports**. The page has two sections.

## Admission and student reports

The **Admission & Student Reports** section has two cards.

| Card | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Student Admissions** | Complete enrolled student records. |
| **Dropouts** | Students marked as dropped out. |

To download a report, click **Download Report** on its card. The file downloads to your device as `Student_Admissions_Report` or `Dropouts_Report`.

## Bulk upload and onboarding templates

The **Bulk Upload & Onboarding Templates** section has two template cards. Each card offers two downloads:

- **Download (.xlsx)**: a blank template for new entries.
- **Export Data (.xlsx)**: a copy of your current active records that you can edit and upload for mass updates.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Student Onboarding Template">
    A multi-sheet workbook with the sheets Students, Course_Fees, Addon_Courses, and Total_Fees.

    - The **New Admissions** option gives you blank sheets for fresh admissions.
    - The **Mass Update** option includes all current active students and their fee structures.

    Upload the completed workbook from the [Admissions](/students/admissions) page.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Books & Curriculum Template">
    A two-sheet workbook with Books_Master and Book_Mappings. Books_Master holds the catalog and stock. Book_Mappings holds the program assignments.

    - The **New Catalog** option gives you a blank stock catalog.
    - The **Mass Update** option includes all current active books, stock, and program assignments.

    Upload the completed workbook from the [Books and stationery](/admin/books-stationary) page.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
