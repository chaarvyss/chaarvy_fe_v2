Chaarvy's Reports section gives administrators ready-to-download summaries of key institutional data. Whether you need a snapshot of student intake for a given period or a record of student dropouts, the Reports page puts that information at your fingertips with a single click.

## Student Admissions Report

The Student Admissions Report consolidates enrollment data across your institution, giving you a structured view of admitted students and their program details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports">
    Go to **Admin → Reports** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Student Admissions Report">
    Find the **Student Admissions** card on the Reports page. This report aggregates student enrollment records across all programs and admission cycles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the report">
    Click the **Student Admissions** card to generate and download the report. Chaarvy will prepare the file and save it to your device, ready to open in a spreadsheet application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the report contents">
    The downloaded file contains student enrollment data including student names, program details, admission dates, and enrollment status. Use this data for internal analysis, accreditation submissions, or stakeholder reporting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dropouts Report

The Dropouts Report provides a focused view of students who have left your institution, helping you track attrition and identify trends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the Dropouts Report">
    On the Reports page, find the **Dropouts** card alongside the other available reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the report">
    Click the **Dropouts** card to generate and download the report. The file will be saved to your device automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the report contents">
    The downloaded file contains records of students who have dropped out, which you can use for internal review, board reporting, or regulatory submissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Downloading the Student Admissions Report requires the `reports:download:studentAdmissionsReport` permission. This is a permission name within Chaarvy's access control system. If the report card is not visible or is disabled on your Reports page, contact your system administrator to have this permission granted to your account or role.
</Note>
