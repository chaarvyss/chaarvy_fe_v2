Use the **Holidays** page to keep a list of your institution's holidays for each calendar year.

Go to **Administration** > **Holidays**.

## Choose a year

At the top of the page, **Calendar Year:** shows chips for the current year, the two years before it, and the two years after it. Click a year to show its holidays. The table title changes to match, for example **Holidays (2025)**.

## View holidays

Three summary cards appear above the table:

- **Total Holidays**
- **Upcoming Holidays**: holidays from today onward.
- **Past Holidays**

The table lists holidays for the selected year in date order.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **S#** | Row number. |
| **Holiday Name** | The name of the holiday. |
| **Date** | The holiday date. |
| **Day of Week** | The weekday the holiday falls on. |
| **Status** | **Today**, **Upcoming**, or **Past**. |
| **Actions** | Edit and delete icons. |

Use the search box to find a holiday by name, date, or weekday.

## Add a holiday

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Add Holiday**. The **Add New Holiday** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name">
    Type a name in **Holiday Name**, for example *Independence Day*. This field is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the date">
    Choose a date in **Holiday Date**. The date defaults to today. The full date and weekday appear below the picker so you can check it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Holiday**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each entry covers one date. For a holiday that spans several days, add one entry per day.

## Edit a holiday

Click the pencil icon (**Edit Holiday**) in the row. Change the name or date in the **Edit Holiday** window, then click **Save Changes**.

## Delete a holiday

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the deletion">
    Click the delete icon (**Delete Holiday**) in the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Holiday** window, click **Delete**. This action can't be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where holidays appear

Holidays show on the [Schedule Planner](/faculty/schedule-planner) calendar. Faculty can't pick a holiday date when they plan a schedule.
