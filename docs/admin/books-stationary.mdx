Chaarvy's Books & Stationery module lets you maintain a centralised inventory of all physical materials your institution provides to students — from textbooks and reference books to notebooks and other stationery. Keeping this list up to date ensures accurate fee breakdowns and smooth resource planning.

## Adding Items

Navigate to **Admin → Books & Stationery** to view and manage your inventory.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Books form">
    Click **Add Books** to open the item creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter item details">
    Fill in the required information:
    - **Book Name** — the full name of the book or stationery item (for example, *Engineering Mathematics Vol. 1* or *A4 Notebook*).
    - **Available Quantity** — the current stock count for this item.
    - **Price** — the unit price charged to students.
    - **Program association** — choose whether the item is a **common** item (available across all programs) or a **specific** item linked to a particular program, segment, and medium.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the item">
    Click **Save** to add the item to your inventory. It will appear in the list immediately and be available for use in program fee configurations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Browsing and Filtering Inventory

The inventory list gives you a snapshot of all items and their current associations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search and filter items">
    Use the search bar and filter options — including program, segment, and medium — to narrow down the list and find specific items quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand item associations">
    Each row in the list shows the book name along with its association: items marked **Common book** are shared across all programs, while other items display the specific program, segment, and medium they belong to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit item details">
    Click the edit icon next to any item to update its name, quantity, price, or program association. Use this to correct errors or reflect changes such as a new edition of a textbook or updated stock levels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can link books and stationery items to specific academic programs so they are automatically included in the admission fee breakdown presented to students. Configure these links when adding or editing an item, or manage them in the Program settings under the Academics section.
</Tip>
