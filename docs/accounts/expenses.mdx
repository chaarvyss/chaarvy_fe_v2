The Expenses module gives your accounts team a single place to record every outgoing payment made by the institution. Whether you're logging a utility bill, a vendor payment, or a staff reimbursement, Chaarvy keeps an organised, searchable history of all expenses alongside their supporting documents.

## Accounts Dashboard

Before diving into individual expense records, the **Accounts Dashboard** gives you a financial snapshot of the institution at a glance. Navigate to **Accounts > Dashboard** to see:

- An **overall expenses chart** that visualises spending trends over time.
- A **financial status overview** summarising income, expenditure, and balance figures.

Use the dashboard as your starting point each time you open the Accounts module — it surfaces the information you need to make quick decisions before drilling into specific records.

## Viewing Expenses

To see all recorded expenses, navigate to **Accounts > Expenses**. The expenses list displays every entry your team has logged, sorted by the most recent date by default.

From the list view you can:

- **Search** by keyword to locate a specific expense quickly.
- **Filter** by expense category, date range, payment mode, or beneficiary type to narrow down results.
- **Click any row** to open the full expense detail and view attached receipts or invoices.

## Adding an Expense

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Expense form">
    From the **Accounts > Expenses** page, click the **Add Expense** button in the top-right corner. A form panel will open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a description and date">
    Fill in the **Description** field to briefly summarise what the expense is for. Then select the **Date** the expense was incurred.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select who paid and the payee">
    Choose the staff member who made the payment from the **Paid By** dropdown. Then select the **Beneficiary Type** that best describes who received the payment, followed by the specific **Payee** within that type. These fields are used for reporting and audit purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an expense category and payment mode">
    Choose the appropriate **Expense Category** from the dropdown, then select the **Payment Mode** (for example, cash, bank transfer, or cheque) that reflects how the payment was made. If the category or payment mode you need does not exist yet, type the new name directly in the dropdown and use the inline **Add** option to create it on the spot — no separate settings page is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reference ID and notes">
    Enter a **Reference ID** (such as a cheque number or transaction reference) for traceability, and add any additional **Notes** relevant to the expense.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Enter the **Amount** paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach receipts or invoices">
    Click **Select Files** to attach supporting documents such as receipts, invoices, or purchase orders. You can attach multiple files in common formats (PDF, JPG, PNG). All attachments are stored securely and can be retrieved at any time from the expense record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the expense">
    Review the details and click **Add Expense**. The new expense immediately appears in the expenses list and contributes to the figures shown on the Accounts Dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing an Expense

To correct or update an existing record, locate the expense in the list and click the **Edit** (pencil) icon. The same form used for adding an expense reopens with the existing values pre-filled. Make your changes and click **Update Expense** to apply them. You can also add or remove file attachments at this point.

<Warning>
  Editing a saved expense changes the figures reflected on the Accounts Dashboard. Always double-check the amount and date before saving an update.
</Warning>

## Expense Categories and Payment Modes

Chaarvy lets you manage expense categories, payment modes, and beneficiary types without leaving the expense form. When filling in any of these dropdown fields, type a name that does not yet exist and select the inline **Add** option that appears — the new entry is saved immediately and becomes available institution-wide for future expenses.

You can also rename any existing category or payment mode by clicking the **Edit** option next to it in the same dropdown, keeping your reference data tidy as the institution's needs change.

<Info>
  Beneficiary types work the same way — you can add or rename them inline from the **Beneficiary Type** dropdown while recording an expense.
</Info>

## Permissions

<Note>
  To add or edit expenses, your account must have the **expenses:add** and **expenses:edit** permissions assigned. To view the expenses list without making changes, the **expenses:view:list** permission is sufficient. Contact your system administrator if you cannot see the Add Expense button or the edit controls.
</Note>
