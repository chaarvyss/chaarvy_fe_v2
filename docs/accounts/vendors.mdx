The **Vendors** page is a directory of the suppliers and service providers your institution works with.

Go to **Accounts** > **Vendors** to open it.

## Vendor list

The list shows these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **#** | The row number. |
| **Vendor Name** | The vendor's name, with their email address below it. |
| **Vendor Firm Name** | The vendor's company or firm name. |
| **Contact Number** | The vendor's contact number. |
| **Actions** | The edit (pencil) icon. |

To find a vendor, type in the search box in the table header.

## Add a vendor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Add vendor**. The **Add Vendor** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the vendor details">
    Fill in all of these required fields:

    | Field | Details |
    |---|---|
    | **Vendor Name** | The name of the vendor or contact person. |
    | **Vendor Firm Name** | The company or firm name. |
    | **Contact Number** | Must be 10 digits. |
    | **Email** | Must be a valid email address. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add vendor**. A confirmation message appears and the vendor is added to the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a vendor

1. Click the edit (pencil) icon in the vendor's row. The **Edit Vendor** panel opens with the current details filled in.
2. Change the details you need.
3. Click **Update vendor**.
