The Vendors module lets your accounts team build and maintain a complete directory of every supplier or service provider your institution works with. Keeping vendor records accurate and up to date makes it easy to reference the right contact when logging purchases, approving payments, or auditing expenditure.

## Viewing Vendors

Navigate to **Accounts > Vendors** to open the vendor list. Every vendor that has been registered in Chaarvy appears here, showing key details such as the vendor name, firm name, and contact information at a glance.

From the list view you can:

- **Search** by vendor name to locate a specific supplier quickly.
- **Click the Edit (pencil) icon** on any row to open and update that vendor's details.

## Adding a Vendor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Vendor form">
    From the **Accounts > Vendors** page, click the **Add Vendor** button in the top-right corner. The vendor form will open in a side panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the vendor name and firm name">
    Fill in the **Vendor Name** (the individual contact or representative name) and the **Vendor Firm Name** (the trading or company name). Both fields are required so that expense records clearly identify who you are dealing with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contact details">
    Enter the vendor's **Contact Number** and **Email** address. Accurate contact details make it easy to reach the supplier when you need to follow up on orders or invoices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the vendor">
    Review all the details and click **Add Vendor**. The vendor is now registered in Chaarvy and appears in the vendor list for use across the Accounts module.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing a Vendor

To update an existing vendor's details, find the vendor in the list and click the **Edit** (pencil) icon on their row. The form reopens with the existing values pre-filled. Make your changes and click **Update Vendor**. Updates take effect immediately and are reflected wherever the vendor is referenced.

<Tip>
  Link your vendor records to expenses whenever you log a payment. Selecting the vendor while recording an expense creates a clear audit trail between what was purchased, who supplied it, and how it was paid — making financial reviews and procurement reports significantly easier to produce.
</Tip>
