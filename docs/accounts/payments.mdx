The **Payments** page lists student fee payment transactions. Use it to review collections, reprint receipts, and open a student's fee record.

To open it, go to **Accounts** > **Payments**.

## Summary cards

- **Total Transactions**: the total number of payment transactions.
- **Filtered Count**: the number of transactions that match your current filters.
- **Amount Collected**: the total amount collected.

## Transactions table

By default, the table shows successful transactions.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **S#** | Row number. |
| **Admission number** | The student's admission number. |
| **Student Name** | The student's name. |
| **Status** | **Success** or **Failed**. |
| **Amount** | The amount paid. |
| **Payment Date** | The date of the payment. |
| **Action** | Icons to print the receipt or open the student's payments. |

Use the pagination controls below the table to move between pages.

## Filter transactions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the filters">
    Click the filter icon in the table header. The **Filters** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your filters">
    - **Search**
    - **Date range**: click **Select date range** and pick a start and end date.
    - **Status**: choose **Successful** or **Failed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filters">
    Click **Search** to apply the filters. Click **Reset** to clear them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Row actions

- **Payment Reciept** (document icon): generates the receipt and opens your browser's print dialog. If it fails, the message **Failed to generate reciept** appears.
- **Payments** (transfer icon): opens [Collect payment](/students/payments) with the student's fee details already loaded.

## Collect a new payment

Click **Collect Payment** in the table header to open the Collect Payment page. For the full steps, see [Collect payment](/students/payments).
